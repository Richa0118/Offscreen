# Offscreen

A small, free desktop app that nudges you to rest your eyes, drink water and move. It
counts the time you are actually at your computer, not the time on the clock, and every
nudge is a different hand-drawn character, so you still notice it in week three.

Mac and Windows. No account, no tracking, no network calls.

## Download

- **Mac** (Apple chip and Intel): [Offscreen-mac.dmg](https://github.com/Richa0118/Offscreen/releases/latest/download/Offscreen-mac.dmg)
- **Windows** (10 and 11): [Offscreen-Setup.exe](https://github.com/Richa0118/Offscreen/releases/latest/download/Offscreen-Setup.exe)

## Installing

Offscreen is free, so it is not paid for with Apple or Microsoft. That is the only reason
your computer warns you the first time.

**Mac**

1. Open `Offscreen-mac.dmg` and drag Offscreen into Applications.
2. Open it. macOS says it "could not verify" Offscreen. Click **Done**.
3. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down, click **Open Anyway** next to
   Offscreen, and confirm with your password or Touch ID.
4. It lives in your menu bar and starts itself at login.

**Windows**

1. Run `Offscreen-Setup.exe`. On "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then
   **Run anyway**.
2. Install. Offscreen lives in the system tray by the clock. If you do not see it, click the
   **^** arrow and drag it out.

## What it sees

One number: how many seconds since the keyboard or mouse was last touched. Not your
screen, files, apps, websites, camera, microphone or clipboard. It sends nothing anywhere.
